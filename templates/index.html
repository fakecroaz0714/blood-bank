MEDICAL GUIDE

Blood Group Compatibility

Understanding blood compatibility is vital for transfusions. O- Negative is the universal donor, whereas AB+ Positive can receive from all groups.

  • O- Negative: Universal Red Cell Donor
  • AB+ Positive: Universal Recipient
  • Donation Interval: 90 Days between whole blood donations
